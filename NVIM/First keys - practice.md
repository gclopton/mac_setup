# First keys — practice

Practice one group at a time. Type the command into the answer box; do not press Escape or other control keys as your answer. Capitalization matters: `V` and `v` are different commands. Each prompt specifies the command form being rehearsed; other ways to perform the task may exist.

After a group, try its commands in `NVIM/practice.txt` inside Neovide. These cards test recall; they do not watch or grade what you do in the editor. No timed exam is involved.
